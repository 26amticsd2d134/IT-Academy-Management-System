01 / Project overview

What the system is for.

The IT Academy Management System is a web-based concept for handling routine academy administration in one organised place.

01

Student management

Maintain student profiles, enrollment details and course information.

02

Course management

Organise course lists, materials and class-related information.

03

Fee management

Record payments and identify fees that still need attention.

04

Progress tracking

Review learning progress and academic performance.

05

Certificates

Track completion and certificate status.

06

Role-based access

Different views can be provided for administrators, teachers and students.

Project objectives

  • Reduce manual record keeping and repeated data entry.
  • Make student and course information easier to find.
  • Improve visibility of fee status and student progress.
  • Keep information organised and accessible to authorised users.
