06 / Requirement engineering

What the system should do.

These functional requirements describe the main expected actions for the academy management system.

FR-01

Student registration

The system shall allow authorised users to add and update student details.

FR-02

Course management

The system shall maintain course details and learning materials.

FR-03

Fee records

The system shall record payments and show pending balances.

FR-04

Progress tracking

The system shall display student progress and assessment status.

FR-05

Certificate management

The system shall track eligibility and certificate issue status.

FR-06

Secure login

The system shall provide sign-in and role-appropriate access.

FR-07

Search records

The system shall help users locate relevant student and course information.

FR-08

Generate summaries

The system shall provide useful summaries of enrolment and fees.

FR-09

Maintain records

The system shall keep records organised and available to authorised users.

No matching requirements found.

Non-functional requirements

Usability: pages should be clear and easy to learn.

Security: private information should only be available to authorised users.

Reliability: records should remain consistent and accurate.

Performance: common searches and actions should feel responsive.
